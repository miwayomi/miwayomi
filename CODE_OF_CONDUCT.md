# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **miwayomi** and
its community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or
  advances.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Project-specific rules (forks and contributions)

Because miwayomi is an **execution engine** for third-party extensions, the
following rules apply to everyone — including fork authors and contributors:

1. **No content distribution.** Do not use miwayomi or any fork to distribute,
   host, or help redistribute copyrighted content, media, or extension
   repositories. miwayomi ships no content itself, and neither should you.
2. **Respect third-party rights.** Respect the copyright of artists, publishers,
   scanlation groups, and site owners. Respect the terms of service of every
   website or service the software interacts with. Do not use this project to
   infringe copyright or bypass access controls you are not authorized to
   bypass.
3. **Extensions are third-party software.** Never redistribute someone else's
   extensions or claim them as your own. If you mention extensions, attribute
   their authors.
4. **Honest provenance.** If you contribute code adapted from other projects,
   say so and comply with their licenses. Original work must not be presented
   as someone else's, and adapted work must not be presented as original.
5. **Forks must keep attribution and disclaimers.** Any fork must retain the
   legal disclaimer and the license attribution (see `NOTICE-ANIYOMI.md`), and
   must not imply affiliation with, endorsement by, or sponsorship from
   miwayomi, Tachiyomi, Aniyomi, or any related project.
6. **No false claims.** Do not use the miwayomi name or branding to imply
   official status, endorsement, or that a fork is the official project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders (maintainers) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
